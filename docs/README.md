# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This guide is the entry point to OctoAcme's project management processes. The documents below describe an end-to-end lifecycle for cross-functional projects, from validating an idea through delivery, release, and continuous improvement.

## Project lifecycle at a glance

1. **Initiate** — Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders and resources, and decide whether the work is ready for planning.
2. **Plan** — Turn the approved initiative into a prioritized backlog and delivery plan, with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified dependencies and risks.
3. **Execute and track** — Deliver in small increments, coordinate through the project board and team cadence, use reviews and testing to maintain quality, and monitor progress against milestones and success metrics.
4. **Manage risks and communication** — Maintain and review the risk register, keep stakeholders informed through a single source of truth, and escalate blockers through the agreed path.
5. **Release and deploy** — Confirm release readiness, run deployment and verification checks, communicate the release, and use the rollback and incident playbook when needed.
6. **Learn and improve** — Hold retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents; assign a small number of actionable improvements and follow up on their impact.
